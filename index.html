Mock exams scored against the real pass mark

Fifty questions, a countdown, flagging and a question palette, and no feedback until you submit, as on exam day. The verdict is pass or fail on the whole paper at 90%. The mix across subject areas is an estimate, and the result shows where your marks went.

Correct to 100 %

The study guide says the PSTAR is corrected to 100 %. After a mock, one tap takes every question you missed and keeps asking until each has been answered correctly once, with the explanation each time you slip. It records nothing but your normal practice answers.

The guide's own questions, and more

The app includes all 185 questions from Transport Canada's study guide, reproduced as printed with their original option order and answer key, and 251 original practice questions written from the current Canadian Aviation Regulations and TC AIM. Each official question is tagged with its number in the guide, and its explanation is checked against the current CARs and TC AIM 2026-2, saying where the guide's own references have since moved. Choose aeroplane or helicopter and the questions that apply only to the other category are left out.

Smart review that doesn't forget

Every answer is tracked. Missed questions return on a spaced schedule until they stick, and the dashboard estimates your readiness against the 90% pass mark, by area and by topic, so you always know what to study next. A Library card per topic holds the must-know points and is always open.

Free, private and offline

PSTAR Prep is free: no purchases, no subscription, no ads, no account, no analytics and no tracking. Everything works offline, your progress stays on your device, and the developer never sees it. For iPhone and iPad.

Your path to the PPL

PSTAR Prep is the first step. The same developer makes companion apps for the radio operator certificate (ROC-A) and for the PPL written exam; the app lists them in order, and each opens its own App Store page.
