PSTAR Prep · iPhone & iPad

Support

A study aid for the PSTAR, the air-regulations written examination for the Canadian student pilot permit or private pilot licence for foreign and military applicants (study guide TP 11919E).

Questions, bug reports, or a question you think is wrong? Email patrguzz@gmail.com. Corrections are especially welcome: please include the question and the reference you're working from (TC AIM section, CAR number, or the question's number in the study guide).

Requirements

iPhone or iPad running iOS 26.0 or later. Studying works entirely offline; the internet is used only if you tap another app in "Your path to the PPL", which opens that app's App Store page.

The app is free

There is nothing to buy, nothing locked, and no code to redeem: every question, mock exam and study card is open from the first launch.

How the app works

Practise by topic with instant feedback: select an option, then Confirm answer to see the explanation and its reference. In Practice, choose All, Official or Original to decide which questions a topic session draws from. In the Library, "I'm training for" lets you choose aeroplane or helicopter; questions that apply only to the other category are left out of practice and the mock exam. Sit the 50-question mock exam under the clock; the pass mark is 90% on the whole paper. On the result screen, Correct to 100 % asks every question you missed, and any you miss again, until each has been answered correctly once. Every answer is tracked: missed questions come back on a spaced schedule in Smart Review, and the Home tab estimates your readiness by area and topic. With a keyboard, A–D or 1–4 choose, Return confirms, and the arrow keys move through a mock exam.

Official questions and rule changes

Questions tagged "Official guide question" are Transport Canada's own, shown with the options in the order the guide prints them and keyed as the guide keys them. The guide is dated December 2022. Every official answer was re-checked against the current CARs and TC AIM 2026-2; where the guide's references have moved, the explanation says so. If a keyed answer ever stops matching the current rule, the question carries a "Rule change since the guide" note: the mock exam marks the guide's key, and the note teaches the current rule.

Progress and resetting

Your progress is saved on your device only. To start over, use "Reset all progress" at the bottom of the Library tab. Deleting the app deletes your progress.

A question looks wrong?

Every question carries an explanation and a source reference. If you believe an answer conflicts with the current TC AIM or CARs, email the question text and your reference: verified corrections ship in the next update. Always treat the current official publications as authoritative.
